No pre-work is required for this workshop.

## Workshop

<LockedContent password="progress">

**Draw** on the board the three pillars of technical progress tracking:

> - Mastery Quiz
> - Technical Assessment
> - Coach Assessment

**Discuss** positives and limitations of each, then **ask** trainees to give an example scenario for when each would highlight a gap.

**Explain** that we track behaviours via:

> - Observations by coaches
> - Automated data (attendance, punctuality, commit history)
> - Peer feedback (MVP nominations, actionable comments)

### Weekly Tracking

**Outline** the flow:

> - Inputs: All of the above
> - Outputs: [A dashboard summarising competency progress](https://sigma-performance-dashboard.streamlit.app/), qualitative feedback, next-week objectives
> - Meetings: weekly 121 in weeks 1-3; fortnightly thereafter (or more often if needed)

**Discuss** how this cycle supports continuous improvement.

### **What is a 121?**

**Explain** that a 121 is:

- A 15-20 minute planned meeting led by the trainee
- A forum to share successes, struggles, future objectives, and mutual feedback
- Not for deep technical debugging or retrospective complaints

**Review** the standard agenda:

- Your planned talking points
- Talk through any feedback you’ve received
- Agreed objectives for next week

**Clarify** cadence:

- Weekly in the first three weeks
- Fortnightly thereafter (unless more frequent check-ins are requested)

**Emphasise** trainee responsibility:

- Prepare talking points beforehand
- Take and share notes after each meeting

### **121 Task**

**Provide** the trainees with a list of sample agenda items:

> A) “I’m struggling with understanding async programming concepts in JavaScript. Can we go through it?”  
> B) “I’m finding it hard to keep up the content. Have you got any advice?”  
> C) “I’m binge-watching the latest Netflix series and stayed up all night, so I might not be very productive today.”  
> D) “I’ve come across a subscription to a coding platform that offers hands-on projects. Can I get access to it?”  
> E) “What else could I be doing to increase my Proactivity score?”  
> F) “The coding tools we use are terrible.”  
> G) “I’ve noticed Gary (another trainee) has been a bit down this week.”  
> H) “Here are my goals for the next week - what do you think?”  
> I) “My dog is really sick, and I’m worried it’s going to affect my work.”  
> J) “I didn’t finish the coursework.”  
> K) “I think you could improve the course by making [this] change.”  
> L) “Here’s a list of things I’ve done that I’m proud of this week.”  
> M) “My code doesn’t work, can you help?”  
> N) “Could I get more information about why my Content Mastery score is low?”  
> O) “There’s been construction work in my house, which is why I didn’t finish the coursework.”  
> P) “I’ve noticed Gary (another trainee) is struggling with problem solving.”  
> Q) “I haven’t finished the coursework, but here is what I’m going to change to ensure it doesn’t happen again.”

**Tell** the trainees, in small groups, sort each item into “Good 121 topic” or “Bad 121 topic.”

**Debrief**: Discuss why certain items are effective and others are not.
